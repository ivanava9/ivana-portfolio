Project Overview

Summary / Context

Analyzed grocery transaction data to evaluate category performance, customer behavior, and pricing effectiveness across the retail business.

Goals

Identify top-performing categories, understand revenue drivers, and evaluate how pricing influences customer purchasing behavior.

Process

Validated and prepared sales data, combined multiple datasets in BigQuery, answered business questions with SQL, and translated findings into business recommendations.

Output

Identified Confections as the top-performing category, contributing 12.87% of revenue with a 99.85% repeat purchase rate. The analysis also showed that pricing had limited influence on customer spending and that revenue was broadly distributed across product categories.

Key Responsibilities

  • Validated sales data and identified data quality issues before analysis.
  • Built SQL queries to analyze category performance and customer purchasing behavior.
  • Evaluated revenue contribution, repeat purchases, and pricing effectiveness across product categories.
  • Translated analytical findings into business recommendations for category and pricing strategy.

Recommendations

  • Increase investment in high-performing product categories such as Confections.
  • Grow basket size through cross-selling and bundled promotions.
  • Prioritize value-based pricing instead of competing on lower prices.
  • Focus marketing efforts on expanding repeat purchases in lower-performing categories.
