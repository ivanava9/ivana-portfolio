Project Overview

Summary / Context

Analyzed credit card transaction and customer data to understand spending behavior, identify business opportunities, and segment customers for targeted marketing.

Goals

Understand customer behavior, identify profitable user segments, and recommend strategies to increase credit card usage.

Process

Cleaned and prepared customer and card datasets, explored transaction and fraud patterns, engineered customer features, and applied RFM segmentation to build actionable customer personas.

Output

Identified eight customer segments with distinct spending and engagement patterns. The analysis highlighted Potential Loyalists and Customers Needing Attention as the best opportunities for increasing transaction frequency and long-term customer value.

Key Responsibilities

  • Cleaned and prepared customer and credit card datasets for analysis.
  • Performed exploratory analysis on transaction activity, fraud, credit limits, and customer financial profiles.
  • Built RFM-based customer segmentation to identify customer personas and engagement levels.
  • Developed targeted business recommendations to improve retention, spending, and credit card usage.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize Potential Loyalists with cashback offers and spending challenges.
  • Re-engage Customers Needing Attention through personalized promotions.
  • Retain Champions and Loyal Customers with premium rewards and referral programs.
  • Launch low-cost campaigns to reactivate At Risk and Hibernating customers.
