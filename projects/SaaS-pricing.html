Project Overview

Summary / Context

PeopleU, a fictional HR SaaS company, wanted to review its subscription prices and promotion strategy after identifying that its prices were above the market. The analysis examined contract growth, GMV, promotions, and price sensitivity across three subscription types.

Goals

Evaluate promotion performance, measure price sensitivity, estimate suitable price ranges, and recommend pricing strategies for Business, Enterprise, and Startup subscriptions.

Process

Analyzed historical contract and GMV data, compared promoted and non-promoted sales, calculated price elasticity, simulated price-demand scenarios, and translated the results into pricing recommendations.

Output

Found that larger discounts did not improve promotion performance. Business and Startup subscriptions showed stronger price sensitivity, while Enterprise demand appeared influenced by factors beyond price. The analysis produced separate pricing and promotion recommendations for each subscription type.

Key Responsibilities

  • Analyzed contract growth, GMV, and promotion performance across 2021–2022.
  • Compared promoted and non-promoted sales to assess whether higher discounts improved acquisition.
  • Calculated price elasticity for Business, Enterprise, and Startup subscriptions.
  • Built price-demand scenarios to estimate revenue-maximizing price ranges.
  • Translated analytical findings into subscription-specific pricing and promotion recommendations.

Recommendations

  • Business: Test a permanent price around 22,500, with targeted promotional pricing around 18,000–18,500.
  • Enterprise: Test a moderate price adjustment around 14,000–14,500 and prioritize referral incentives over broad discounts.
  • Startup: Test a permanent price around 18,500 while retaining approximately 16,000 for promotional experiments.
  • Promotions: Replace blanket discounts with targeted and measurable campaigns.
  • Customer acquisition: Strengthen referral and organic acquisition where customer economics are more attractive.
